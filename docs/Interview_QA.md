# Project 2 — Interview Q&A

### 1. Explain the project.
“My second project is Branch Banking Operations and Customer Service Analytics. I analyzed customer service requests, branch workload, staff availability, waiting time, turnaround time, SLA performance, rework and escalation. I used SQL and Python for analysis and Power BI for the management dashboard. The goal was to identify operational bottlenecks and understand how branch operations can support better customer service.”

### 2. Why this project?
“I wanted a project closer to banking operations rather than a purely technical model. It helped me connect customer experience, process efficiency, capacity and business outcomes.”

### 3. What is TAT?
“TAT means Turnaround Time. In this project it represents the total time taken for a request, including waiting and service time, with modeled additional delay where rework occurs.”

### 4. What is SLA?
“SLA is the expected service time target. I compared TAT with the service-specific SLA target to classify each request as met or breached.”

### 5. Why SQL?
“I used SQL for aggregation and operational questions such as branch performance, service TAT, hourly workload, SLA compliance and rework rates.”

### 6. Why Python?
“I used Python mainly for data validation and reproducible analysis. It helped me check the dataset and verify KPIs before dashboarding.”

### 7. What did you find?
“The main patterns were higher pressure around 11 AM to 1 PM, longer TAT for more complex services such as loan inquiry and account opening, and higher documentation/rework rates for KYC/account-opening processes.”

### 8. What would you recommend?
“I would investigate peak-hour capacity allocation, review document-heavy process steps, and compare low-SLA branches with similar-volume peers. Recommendations would be validated against actual bank processes before implementation.”

### 9. Is the data real?
“No. The dataset is simulated for a portfolio project because real branch-level operational data is proprietary. I defined the operational relationships and validated the resulting patterns through analysis.”

### 10. Did you prove that low staffing causes delays?
“No. I observed associations in the simulated data. I would not claim causation without controlled operational evidence or a real-world study.”

### 11. How is this relevant to ICICI?
“It connects directly with branch operations, customer engagement, service quality and business ownership. It also gives me a practical way to discuss sales and relationship signals without treating them as automatic recommendations.”

### 12. What is one limitation?
“The biggest limitation is that the data is simulated. A production version would need actual branch event logs, official SLAs, staffing rosters and validated compliance definitions.”
